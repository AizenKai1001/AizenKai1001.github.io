16 dossiersA RESEARCH NOTEBOOK, WITH ITS SOURCES
R01 / Learning & memory↗

Developmental Intelligence Without Neural Networks

A research program around executable distinctions, bounded program induction, counterexamples, and reusable symbolic structure. The aim is to study developmental learning mechanisms without treating neural networks or language-model priors as the default substrate, while keeping novelty claims separate from implemented evidence.

Research program3 primary sources
R02 / Learning & memory↗

Predictive States, Causal States & Discovering Structure

A study of predictive state representations, computational mechanics, and active automata learning as alternatives to storing a hidden-state vector whose meaning is implicit. The common theme is to define state by what it predicts about future observations and interventions.

Research notes3 primary sources
R03 / Learning & memory↗

Continual Learning, Plasticity & a Negative Result

Research into why networks can lose the ability to learn new tasks, paired with a controlled local benchmark that did not reproduce the headline performance decline at its tested scale. Internal degradation appeared under Adam, but it did not translate into worse task learning.

Measured study3 primary sources
R04 / Embodied intelligence↗

Connectomes, Embodied Agents & What Wiring Does Not Prove

A literature-and-experiment thread around fruit-fly connectomes, visual circuits, embodied simulation, and plasticity. The research treats a connectome as anatomical structure rather than a learned policy, and asks which additional physiology, learning rules, sensors, and validation tasks are required for behavior.

Research notes4 primary sources
R05 / AI systems↗

Memory Beyond the Context Window

A broad study of retrieval-augmented generation, persistent agent memory, test-time adaptation, fast weights, and context management. The through-line is to distinguish external retrieval from learned state and to measure whether any memory mechanism improves answers rather than merely increasing available context.

Research notes4 primary sources
R06 / AI systems↗

Agent Loops, Tool Use & Progressive Disclosure

Research on the engineering around language models: ReAct-style loops, tool selection, Agent Skills, Model Context Protocol, validation, approval boundaries, and context budgets. The focus is less on “autonomy” as a label and more on where control, state, permissions, and stopping conditions actually live.

Research notes4 primary sources
R07 / AI systems↗

Evaluation That Can Survive a Better Explanation

A methodology thread built from benchmark literature and several local failures: use fixed yardsticks, negative controls, frozen evaluation, independent baselines, and re-runnable measurements. Two local simulations produced apparently positive results that disappeared when the measurement or physics assumptions were tightened.

Measured methodology3 primary sources
R08 / Embodied intelligence↗

World Models & Predictive Representation Learning

A literature study of agents that learn compact predictive representations and use imagined rollouts for planning. Recent video-prediction systems make the visual world itself part of the learned dynamics, raising the question of what an agent must predict to support action rather than merely realistic frames.

Research notes3 primary sources
R09 / AI systems↗

Low-Bit Inference, Quantization & What Accuracy Costs

Research into post-training quantization, activation-aware weight scaling, second-order weight reconstruction, GGUF deployment, and KV-cache compression. The practical question is not simply “how many bits?” but which tensors can lose precision while preserving task behavior on constrained local hardware.

Research notes4 primary sources
R10 / AI systems↗

GPU Execution, Memory Bandwidth & Kernel Reality

A systems-level study of SIMT execution, warps, occupancy, memory coalescing, shared memory, cache behavior, divergence, and roofline thinking. It connects model inference and rendering performance to the hardware’s actual scheduling and memory hierarchy rather than treating advertised FLOPs as the whole machine.

Research notes4 primary sources
R11 / Learning & memory↗

Learning Systems From First Principles

A code-reading and implementation track around automatic differentiation, optimizers, transformer internals, recurrent-depth language models, and small educational frameworks. ForgeGrad implements a compact autograd stack; LLM Forge implements a tokenizer, looped model, and training pipeline while its broader research goals remain open.

Implementation + research5 primary sources
R12 / Worlds & graphics↗

Image-to-3D Reconstruction on Local Hardware

A deferred research plan for reconstructing 3D objects from one or a few images on constrained local hardware. The plan starts by benchmarking pretrained reconstruction methods and data/evaluation pipelines before training anything, then isolates a single trainable component only if the baseline exposes a clear gap.

Experiment planning4 primary sources
R13 / Worlds & graphics↗

Immersive Web Graphics Without Losing the Web

A teardown-and-build thread around single-canvas WebGL, scroll synchronization, animation staging, physical materials, performance budgets, and progressive enhancement. The portfolio’s Three.js work draws from these references while preserving native scrolling and original models instead of copying any creator’s scene or assets.

Research + implementation4 primary sources
R14 / Foundations↗

Dendrites, Memory & Biological Computation

A neuroscience study of multi-compartment dendrites, memory systems, replay, neuromodulated plasticity, and sparse distributed representations. The portfolio uses this material to generate engineering questions, while keeping biological mechanisms, computational models, and artificial neural-network analogies explicitly separate.

Research notes4 primary sources
R15 / Foundations↗

Time, Entropy & Information

A foundations-of-physics synthesis connecting statistical mechanics, the thermodynamic arrow of time, low-entropy boundary conditions, information, and the physical cost of irreversible computation. The note also marks the unresolved step: why the early universe occupied such an unusually low-entropy state.

Research notes3 primary sources
R16 / Foundations↗

Spacetime, Gravity & the Problem of Time

A study of general relativity as dynamical spacetime geometry, experimental tests of equivalence and gravitational waves, and the conceptual problem that canonical quantum gravity has no ordinary external time parameter. It is a map of established theory and open questions, not a proposed replacement for GR.
