Learning by being wrong

What an executable hypothesis buys you—and what it still assumes.

A WORKING NOTE

An observation from the lab, with its assumptions left visible.

Distinction AI ↗

An incorrect prediction is useful evidence. In Distinction AI, it can eliminate a candidate program or make a more specific explanation preferable. Because the hypotheses are executable and small, the revision is directly inspectable.

Start with a prediction

The experiment records an answer before applying the action and measuring the outcome. Training accuracy and frozen evaluation remain separate. This prevents a corrected answer from being reported as if it had been predicted correctly the first time.

Make the assumptions visible

The initial visual adapter supplies segmentation and contact measurements. The learner discovers a rule over those measurements, not the whole perceptual system. Removing a helpful measurement is therefore a necessary control, not an optional footnote.

Keep the next step separate

Discovering its own useful measurements is the next research question. Checkpointing, program naming, and successful small-task prediction are implemented steps, but none alone answers that question.
